Privacy Policy

traumababies — Effective October 7, 2026

Who we are

traumababies is a trade name of Bisbee LLC. We build apps that respect your data, your time, and your right to be left alone.

What we believe

People should be compensated for their creativity and hard work. But information that benefits billions of people should not be hoarded by a few. We build apps that free useful information while protecting the people who use them.

How we handle your data

We design apps that work without collecting your personal data wherever possible. When an app does need to handle information, we collect only what is necessary, store it securely, and never sell it. Each app has its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or AI training.
  • We do not collect more data than each app needs to function.
  • We do not read or access your personal content beyond what is strictly necessary.

Data protection

  • Encryption in transit: All communication uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Encryption at rest: Data stored on our systems is encrypted using industry-standard encryption.
  • Access controls: Only authorized personnel can access systems that handle user data.
  • Data minimization: We collect only what is necessary for each app to function.
  • User-owned storage: Wherever possible, your data stays in your own accounts, not on our servers.

Children
